========================================================================
PROJECT 30: HOTEL BOOKING BUSINESS INSIGHTS & STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
========================================================================

1. OCCUPANCY & CANCELLATION INSIGHTS:
   - Overall cancellation rate stands at 27.49% (24,025 canceled reservations).
   - City Hotel experiences a significantly higher cancellation rate (30.0%) compared to Resort Hotel (23.5%).
   - Recommendation: Implement non-refundable deposit requirements for peak summer dates and high-risk booking channels.

2. REVENUE OPTIMIZATION:
   - Total booked revenue is $34,461,003.11, with $22,976,960.13 realized from completed check-outs.
   - Peak revenue occurs in July and August with ADR climbing above $140/night.
   - Dynamic pricing should be deployed to raise weekend ADR during high-demand summer months.

3. DISTRIBUTION & BOOKING CHANNELS:
   - The dominant channel is TA/TO (accounting for 79.1% of volume).
   - Direct bookings deliver the highest profit margin due to zero OTA commission fees.
   - Recommendation: Launch direct-booking loyalty perks (complimentary breakfast/parking) to migrate OTA customers.

4. DEMOGRAPHICS & CUSTOMER PROFILES:
   - Primary market nationality: PRT (31.4%), followed by GBR, FRA, ESP, and DEU.
   - Average stay duration is 3.63 nights (Resort: 4.3 nights, City: 3.0 nights).
   - Room Type A is by far the most popular room tier, generating over 70% of total bookings.
